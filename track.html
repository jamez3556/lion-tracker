LION LOGISTICS — Cargo Tracking

Track any air shipment (AWB), sea container or Bill of Lading

AWB — airline is detected from the 3-digit prefix  ·  Container — shipping line is detected from the 4-letter owner code  ·  BL — line detected from the BL prefix. Where the carrier supports it the tracker opens with your number pre-filled; otherwise the number is copied — just paste it.

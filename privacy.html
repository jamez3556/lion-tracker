Privacy Policy

LION Logistics Clearance Tracker · last updated 31 July 2026

The LION Clearance Tracker is private software used by LION Logistics Pvt Ltd, a customs clearing agency in Malé, Republic of Maldives, to run its own shipment clearance and billing work. It is not sold, licensed or offered to the public.

Who this covers

Two groups of people: our own staff, who sign in to the application, and our customers, whose shipment details we hold in order to clear their cargo and invoice them.

What we hold

  • Shipment records — air waybill and bill of lading numbers, customs declaration numbers, weights, package counts, container numbers, dates and clearance status.
  • Customer records — company name, customs client number, contact details and billing email.
  • Documents uploaded by our staff for a shipment, such as bills of lading, delivery orders and airport or port invoices.
  • Billing information — invoice numbers, amounts, and whether an invoice is paid.

We do not collect card numbers, bank credentials or any payment instrument.

QuickBooks Online

With the permission of the account owner, this application connects to our own QuickBooks Online company to create invoices and to read invoice totals and outstanding balances, so that the amounts shown to staff match the accounts. It reads and writes only our own company's data. It does not access any other QuickBooks company, and it never shares QuickBooks data with a third party. The connection can be withdrawn at any time from within QuickBooks or by using the disconnect address published with the application, after which the stored authorisation is deleted.

Where the data lives

Records are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the asia-south1 region, and supporting services run on Vercel. Access requires an account we create; there is no public sign-up.

What customers can see

A customer may be given a private link showing only their own shipments, and only those documents we have deliberately marked as shared. The link contains a random key and is not indexed or listed anywhere. Anyone holding the link can open that page, so we share it only with the customer.

Sharing

We do not sell data and we do not share it for advertising. Information is disclosed only to the service providers that operate the software (Google, Vercel, Intuit/QuickBooks), to the customer it belongs to, and to Maldives Customs, Maldives Airports Company Limited or Maldives Ports Limited where a clearance requires it.

Keeping it

Clearance and billing records are kept as long as Maldivian tax and customs record-keeping rules require, and then deleted or archived.

Your rights

A customer may ask what we hold about them, ask for a correction, or ask us to stop sharing their status link. Write to the address below and we will respond.

LION Logistics Clearance Tracker · last updated 31 July 2026

1. What this software is

The LION Clearance Tracker is private, internal software owned and operated by LION Logistics Pvt Ltd ("LION"), a customs clearing agency in Malé, Republic of Maldives. It is used by LION's own staff to record customs clearances, track containers and prepare invoices. It is not sold, resold, licensed or made available to the public.

2. Who may use it

Only people to whom LION has issued an account: employees and authorised contractors. Accounts may not be shared. A customer of LION may additionally be given a read-only link showing their own shipments; that link carries no account and grants no other access.

3. Permitted use

Accounts are granted for the purpose of LION's clearing and billing operations. Users must not attempt to reach data belonging to another company, share their credentials, or use the software to break Maldivian law or the rules of Maldives Customs, Maldives Airports Company Limited or Maldives Ports Limited.

4. QuickBooks connection

Where the software is connected to QuickBooks Online, it acts only within LION's own QuickBooks company, with the authorisation of the account owner, to create invoices and read invoice balances. The owner may withdraw that authorisation at any time; the software then stops reading QuickBooks and the stored authorisation is deleted.

5. Data

Shipment and customer records entered into the software belong to LION and, where applicable, to the customer concerned. Handling of that information is described in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of this agreement.

6. Availability

The software is provided as-is for internal use. LION does not guarantee uninterrupted operation and may change or withdraw any part of it. Users remain responsible for checking that customs deadlines and invoice amounts are correct before acting on them.

7. Ending access

LION may withdraw an account at any time, and will do so when a person leaves the company.

8. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Maldives.
